# v0.2 — privacy, communication, logistics and naval expansion

## Solo and multiplayer

The creation screen offers **Solo** (one human, all other governments AI) and **Online multiplayer** (two, three or four humans, unclaimed governments AI). Solo starts immediately and rejects other players joining. Multiplayer uses a private room code and separate browser sessions. Every player must connect to the same running server. No Internet hostname or public deployment has been created yet.

## Information visibility

You see exact personnel/equipment in your own territory, your own fleets and your own deployed reconnaissance teams. Human and AI opponents are hidden by the same server-side projection. Hosting a room does not grant omniscient access. Diplomatic alliances do not automatically disclose exact forces.

A scouting directive costs $100M and produces one private personnel range at resolution (roughly ±25%, rounded). It expires after the next planning month. It does not become a live feed. A special-forces directive costs $200M and 20 fuel, uses an adjacent friendly origin, and commits 100–500 actual personnel. Those personnel cannot also move or attack. Three monthly observations provide narrower personnel and equipment ranges; then the team returns to a friendly region. If all friendly territory is lost before return, the team is captured. Team missions are abstract game reconnaissance; tactical real-world procedures are not modeled.

Observation timestamps, source, confidence and expiration appear in Intelligence and the atlas. Enemy ownership changes invalidate observations. Historical briefings retain the dated information the player actually collected. AI attack selection now uses its own collected reports.

## Official communication

Use **Official Messages** or type a directive such as:

```text
send an official message to China: ask for trade talks next month without making any payment commitment
```

Templates: general communication, trade, alliance, peace, warning and assistance. Automatic drafting converts your stated request into a formal letter. Review/edit the subject and body, then **Confirm & send message**. Delivery is immediate during planning and private to the two governments. Replies preserve the thread reference; sent/received archives and the next monthly briefing record delivery.

AI recipients immediately provide a deterministic diplomatic acknowledgment. Their acknowledgment never signs an agreement or transfers resources. For a general conversation with an AI government, use AI Conversations. Human-to-human free-form communication uses official messages. Message requests are deduplicated; repeat delivery of a retried request is prevented. Ten official messages per government per month are allowed.

Without a configured model, drafting uses faithful templates and explicitly says so. No letter is sent outside this game.

## Logistics

Each starting owned region has a depot. New depots cost $500M. Supplies travel through friendly region adjacency only, using shortest routes; no automatic military-access treaty routing is implemented. Infrastructure and distance bound route capacity, and edges share a 500-unit monthly maximum across deliveries. Allocation is deterministic by region ID; it is not an optimized multi-commodity flow solver.

Per-region monthly requirements:

- Fuel: ceil(tanks ×0.5 + fighters ×3)
- Ammunition: ceil(personnel /400 + tanks ×0.2)
- Spare parts: ceil((tanks + fighters) /10)
- General supplies: ceil(personnel /100)

Delivery is limited by route capacity and national stockpiles. The lowest fulfilled category gives the supply ratio; combat power uses a 0.25 +0.75 × ratio multiplier. Severe shortages reduce readiness and record tank breakdowns. Field-operation fuel/ammunition is additional. New monthly output replenishes stockpiles after operations. The logistics table and supply map layer show your known routes, ratios and bottlenecks.

A friendly logistics/transport vessel at an isolated coastal destination can provide abstract convoy delivery from a friendly coastal depot. A hostile blockade at that destination blocks this delivery. Convoy shipping paths, cargo loading, detailed sea interdiction and territorial encirclement remain simplified. Special-forces missions pay national personnel upkeep but do not have an individually routed supply path yet.

## Naval warfare

Eight types exist: frigates, destroyers, submarines, carriers, patrol vessels, transports, amphibious vessels and logistics ships. Purchase orders consume treasury, occupy production slots and commission real ships only on their due month. Major powers start with one fleet; all naval counts are private.

Each fleet can reposition, engage opposing fleets or blockade a foreign coastal region for one month. Movement requires a connected coast or an explicitly declared sea lane. Operations consume fuel; naval combat also consumes ammunition. Combat uses class power/readiness plus seeded randomness and applies actual ship losses. Fleets require monthly maintenance. Blockades affect convoy supply; ships cannot capture territory by themselves.

This is an abstract naval game system. Carrier aircraft wings, damage states, depth/sonar, amphibious troop lift, full sea-area geography and landings are still unfinished. The current AI-government policy does not autonomously plan sophisticated naval campaigns.

## General AI conversations

The conversation UI supports the presidential adviser and AI-government representatives. It stores private conversation history and supplies only authorized facts. Foreign representative prompts never receive private troop counts or national stockpiles. Responses are advisory/dialogue only, with no game-action tools.

To activate live language-model responses, set `OPENAI_API_KEY` and `OPENAI_MODEL` in the **server environment**, then restart. Choose a text-capable model available to your API account. Do not paste keys in chat or client files. A ChatGPT subscription is not configured as this game's API account. No credentials are bundled and no live paid model call was made during validation.

The optional adapter uses the OpenAI Responses API with `store: false`, a timeout and bounded output. Missing credentials or provider errors result in visibly labeled deterministic fallback text. Message drafts are checked for a valid bounded subject/body; factual fidelity of a generated draft still requires the player's review. Prompt instructions are not a guarantee of perfect model behavior, so generated text never creates mechanical commitments.

Implementation reference: [official OpenAI text-generation documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/text), retrieved during implementation. No fixed model name is assumed; the host configures it.
