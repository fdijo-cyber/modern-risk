# v0.6 — Illustrated board and MUN crisis adjudication

## Current interface

Exactly five main tabs, in order:

1. Map & Public News: 42 original geographical territory paths beneath an illustrated tabletop presentation, terrain/paper/ocean artwork, cartoon infantry/tanks/fighters/bombers/surface ships/submarines/special forces/bases/fortifications, public control and national flags, private formation inventories and movement arrows. Pointer/touch pan and pinch/wheel zoom use a stable SVG and animation frames. Owner-color transitions and contested outlines indicate resolved operations. Troop data stays server filtered. One naval group comprises two surface ships and one submarine; subs are not independently commanded. The optional side link opens the Spotify result “War songs” by UboatXVIII, without autoplay, embedding, account access or library changes.
2. Presidential Office: single multi-paragraph directive composer; confirmation authorizes costs. Same-round analysis, actual per-clause results, resources, unit inventories, national reserves, persistent project progress/history, policy archive. AI cost/risk/timing assessments appear with the adjudicated decision; there is no separate pre-spend review wizard.
3. Foreign Affairs: human/AI conversations and bilateral relationships; formal inbox with accept, reject and counteroffer. Routine AI treaty decisions are immediate. Transfers, loans, trade income, peace and alliance effects remain state-backed.
4. Bank: actual cash, inflows/expenditure, investments, debt, trade/production/project income, oil/fuel/supplies/ammunition, operating obligations and complete month-filtered ledger. Monthly opening + inflows − expenditure = closing, including spending performed before month advancement.
5. Announcements & Reports: WNN public newspaper and authorized confidential edition, reusable original cartoon illustrations, country flags, fictional month dates, round/topic filters and full-article dialogs. Public articles use public committed events. Private directives, scouting, own casualties and presidential briefings are projected only to their country.

## Two layers

The Game Master receives the player's authorized view, public geography, own resources/forces/projects/capabilities and bilateral correspondence. Gemini/OpenAI returns an analysis and structured proposals. It has no database connection or arbitrary code execution.

The engine validates actual inventories, resource costs, ownership, adjacency, bases, debt ceilings and treaties. New action/policy/building labels can be revised into a generic `initiative`, rather than being refused solely for not having a dedicated handler. Each clause settles independently; an unavailable military commitment does not erase an affordable institution. Idempotent HTTP submission receipts prevent a repeated request from charging twice. All result state is saved using the existing revision-checked SQLite transaction.

Generic initiatives preserve arbitrary names, intents and categories. They record target/site, visibility, budget, duration, effect lifetime, operating costs, assessed risk, rationale, implementation progress, delivery outcome and history. They are a resolved decision with continuing consequences, not an unprocessed directive.

Supported effect primitives are economic/political/social/research/counterintelligence/readiness scores, funded productivity income, and fortification levels. The engine normalizes and bounds effects by the paid budget. Unsupported direct mutations (free cash, troops, ownership, arbitrary database fields) are removed/revised with a recorded explanation. Score effects cannot be applied twice by suspending and resuming a completed program. Productivity income is term-limited. Site loss and insufficient operating funds suspend work/benefits. Completed/failed outcomes and articles follow the seeded delivery assessment, not invented narrative successes.

Foreign sites require a cooperation authorization. Human hosts decide in their inbox; AI hosts evaluate war status, security scope and openness. Unauthorized military access and sovereignty changes are never granted through an investment. Pending funding is held as escrow and refunded if declined or expired. Explicit unknown conditions can become an unverified conditional mandate with a three-month escrow expiry instead of being silently treated as true.

## Validation

Automated tests cover same-round narrative adjudication, partial failures, persistence/reload, bounded novel effects, funding and group conservation, private projections, host consent/refunds, initiative progression, duplicate-effect prevention, inaccessible sites, treaty counteroffers/transfers and monthly ledger reconciliation. Older engine and multiplayer/SQLite regressions remain.

A live Gemini call was verified using an imaginative academy + secret observatory + message directive: three valid proposals, two persistent initiatives, $305 spent, round unchanged, secret absent from the other government view. No API key is present in source. Browser QA verified immediate decisions, named locations/confidentiality, project progress at month advancement, actual ledger rows, public/private newspaper editions and full article opening.

## Artwork status and remaining limits

All shipped map/unit/newspaper artwork is original reusable SVG, requiring no external image request. Higgsfield was explicitly requested for new miniatures and its native generation was attempted after a 0.5-credit quote. The account returned `Requires basic plan or higher.` No job started or asset was produced; no Higgsfield asset is claimed. The nine-type vector set stays in use until account access permits generation.

The project is now extensible rather than a command-only prototype, but it is not an unrestricted simulation of every real-world mechanism. Effects use the documented general primitives; new ideas may be represented at that level. There are no independently commanded submarines, a detailed convoy/transport simulation in this simplified engine, or full dynamic international-reaction storytelling. The illustrated board remains stylized geographical vectors, not licensed Risk art. Dense labels are best viewed with zoom. External triggers are not fabricated. No public server/domain is deployed and physical iPad FPS remains unmeasured.
