# v0.4 — directive-first redesign

This user-requested redesign supersedes the previous ministry-heavy UI and large monetary scales. The original master specification remains historical reference, not a claim that its entire roadmap is implemented.

## Four tabs

- **Map:** visible grouped forces, interactive selection/pan/zoom, optional economic/chokepoint markers, contextual scouting shortcut, world news. The shortcut fills the Office draft rather than executing anything.
- **Presidential Office:** the only gameplay-directive composer; long text, conditions, parsed action/cost review, disclosed assumptions, editable quantities/locations/budgets, approval/rejection, private monthly briefing and archived results.
- **Bank:** current/reserved/available dollars, oil and fuel costs, separate funding authorization/rejection, loans, installments, recurring commitments and paid ledger. No separate order composer.
- **Foreign Affairs:** private messenger-style negotiation with AI/human governments, proposed agreement extraction and terms. Review/consent is routed to the Office and Bank. Sending a conversation message costs $5 immediately and appears in the ledger; retries with the same request ID do not charge twice.

## Units, buildings and monthly economy

There are exactly six group types and seven building types in `simple-rules.js`. Costs are integer game dollars. Every government begins with $1,500, 100 oil, 100 fuel; each government receives 30–32 total groups in a country-specific mix, split between regional deployment and national reserve. Starting headquarters receive an economic center, air base, military factory and, where coastal, a naval base. Holdings differ by scenario, so this is not a balanced competitive release.

Infantry = 1,000 people, armor = 120 tanks, bombers = 6 aircraft, fighter jets = 12 aircraft, warships = 3 vessels, special forces = 100 personnel. Map counters total groups; the selected region lists its type breakdown. Internal compatibility fields derive from these groups and cannot be independently spent.

Monthly income = owned regions × $35 + floor(economic score / 2) if land remains + economic centers × $40 + ports × $30 + controlled strategic-site bonuses + active trade agreements × $25. Unit upkeep is ceil(total groups × $1.5); buildings have catalog maintenance. Oil and fuel regenerate from regions/refineries. Resource spending is committed in the ledger and stockpiles during resolution.

Naval/air/armor recruitment requires the appropriate base/factory. This simplified edition treats groups abstractly; it does not provide the previous advanced fleet-operation interface or physical route-capacity logistics UI. The prior advanced engine remains separately available for old saved matches.

## Approval lifecycle

`review → awaiting_bank → funded → executed`, or `clarification`, `rejected`, `expired`.

An interpretation is a stored proposal, not an order execution. Office approval does not spend money. Bank authorization revalidates the proposed costs and reserves dollars/oil/fuel/unit groups against all already funded directives. Rejecting a funded directive releases those reservations. Funds are charged only for executed monthly actions. Loan proceeds cannot be spent in the same planning phase before the loan resolves.

Unfunded proposals expire at month-end with an explicit no-spend result. Funded operations with unmet guards also produce a no-spend result. Individual actions may fail if territorial ownership or valid resources change; the reason is recorded. Monthly maintenance and loan installments are separate recurring charges.

Current guards: treasury_at_least, owns(region), no_treaty(country). In the current representation, guards apply to the entire directive. The offline interpreter discloses that scope. Split independent actions into separate directives. More complex IF/ELSE structures require clarification and are not silently discarded.

## Prose interpretation

A configured server-side model receives only the player's authorized context, public region names and the balance catalog. It emits proposed action JSON with assumptions/conditions/questions. The server validates actions and computes costs itself, ignoring any invented model price. Manual edits recalculate costs and reset Office approval.

Offline mode can recognize common natural wording, region names, basic quantities and conditions, buildings/scouts, messages and loans. Missing nonmaterial details get explicit defaults; material ambiguities ask questions. Broad or multi-layer narratives need the live model for interpretation. No live-provider credentials are bundled, and no paid live call was made in verification.

Arbitrary political, social, economic and intelligence ideas can be proposed as bounded funded policy programs; currently they improve the corresponding modeled score. That does not implement every possible narrative mechanism. Currency reform does not create a separate real gold-backed monetary economy; cyber themes remain abstract intelligence programs. The interpreter cannot execute unmodeled mechanics or real-world operational instructions.

## Negotiated contracts

Messages are private to the parties. AI dialogue uses a live model when configured, otherwise a disclosed generic acknowledgment. Agreement extraction reads the last 30 exchanged messages. Clear terms become an offered contract and Office proposal, not an activated deal.

Supported agreements: trade, aid, loan, peace, alliance. Direction, amount and duration are shown in the interpreted action and contract. Each human party needs its own Office approval and Bank authorization. AI consent is deterministic and remains subject to human approval. A signed-in chat user cannot make the other human consent. After both consents commit in monthly resolution, transfers and recurring effects activate; failed funding cannot mint money. Loans create principal and monthly repayments. Aid/loan payer direction is explicit.

The extractor does not infer an unspecified aid/loan amount. Offline trade duration defaults to six months and is disclosed. Revise conflicting terms before submission.

## Performance and writing

The map retains its SVG nodes between remote updates. Camera updates are scheduled once per animation frame. No SVG filters, drop shadows, textures, or triple path layers are used by the active renderer. Region selection only updates counts/selection and the compact inspector.

Five-second revision-aware polling returns 204 when unchanged. Remote data patches header/list elements and never replaces the directive textarea. Drafts survive tab changes, network updates, refreshes and month advancement through per-room local storage. A second tab's message/contract updates were tested while preserving the Office draft. This is not a measured FPS guarantee on all devices; physical iPad/Safari performance remains to be verified.

## Compatibility

New matches default to the simplified rules. Old room snapshots are preserved and do not silently convert billions, equipment or existing treaties. The compact client offers a new-match path for the new rules; the previous advanced API remains for legacy regression compatibility. Database backups are recommended before upgrading. No hosting/domain change is included.

## Force roster and national advantages

Office displays each region’s infantry, tank, special-force, fighter, warship and bomber groups, plus unallocated reserves and national totals. Initial national inventories are documented in README.md and encoded in seedSimple; every government (including AI) starts at 30–32 groups total. Groups in reserve cannot be used as regional forces without a funded mobilization order. Mobilization preserves total inventory, removes groups from reserve, and adds them to the target only at resolution; standing down performs the inverse. Funding reservations prevent duplicate allocation. Reserves cost $0.50 per group monthly versus $1.50 for deployed groups (rounded in aggregate).

The United States has a 10% fighter/bomber power bonus; the European Coalition has 10% monthly domestic income; China has 10% recruitment discount; Russia has 15% tank power. Minor profiles grant infantry power, naval power or income bonuses. Advantages change actual authoritative costs/outcomes rather than only labels. Income currently applies the bonus to the combined modeled monthly income including trade/site receipts.
